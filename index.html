9:00 AM Freedom Trail Walking Tour Walk the 2.5-mile red brick trail covering 16 historical sites. Freedom Trail Wear good walking shoes! Key stops: Granary Burying Ground, Old State House, Paul Revere House.
12:30 PM Faneuil Hall & Quincy Market Lunch stop inside historic food hall. Faneuil Hall Famous spot for Boston chowder served in bread bowls.
2:30 PM USS Constitution & Charlestown Visit "Old Ironsides" in the historic Navy Yard. USS Constitution Valid government ID required for adults to board the active navy vessel.
6:30 PM Seaport District Dinner Waterfront dining and stroll. — Modern harbor views and fresh seafood along the harbor walk.
